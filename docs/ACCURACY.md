# Accuracy and evidence
This reconstruction represents the school shown on seven presentation sheets in ten photographs. It is an interpretation of those boards, not an official design model, measured survey, construction document, or as-built record.

## What the photographs support
The plans establish program, approximate room boundaries, the arrangement of wings, courtyard and entrance recesses, upper-level voids and roof areas, and the relative location of the childcare floor. Elevation and perspective boards support broad massing, material families and glazing character. Duplicates were used for visual corroboration, not counted as additional floors.

## Coordinate and dimension assumptions
The common horizontal frame follows Photo 8. X points right and Z points down in that photograph. Geometry is scaled at an assumed 0.14 metres per image pixel. This visual calibration is not a measured dimension. All displayed areas and exported metric geometry inherit that uncertainty.
The assumed level elevations are −4.2, 0.0, 4.2 and 8.4 metres. Typical roof elevation is 12.6 metres. Floor-to-floor heights, parapets, wall thicknesses, door widths and window modules are reconstructed estimates.
True north is estimated from the photographed compass arrow as approximately 40 degrees clockwise from drawing-up. Sixth Line is on the drawing's right and Burnhamthorpe Road at the bottom. The model is not georeferenced.

## Evidence labels
- Traced: plan-space boundary and label interpreted from a photograph. This does not mean its dimensions are verified.
- Inferred: placement, subdivision, height or detail estimated from visual evidence or architectural convention.
- Unresolved: evidence insufficient to determine the actual condition.

## Material limitations
Photo perspective causes residual plan distortion. Upper floors require registration to the ground-floor frame. Level 0's oblique photograph gives lower confidence than the other plans.
Only north and south elevations plus one street perspective were supplied. Repetitive window treatments on other faces, concealed construction, furnishings, landscape species, roof plant and minor doors are schematic.
No structural, MEP, fire-rating, accessibility, code-compliance, grading or survey verification is claimed.
