# Lower-level tracing evidence

All dimensions, elevations, heights and metric scale are inferred from undimensioned, oblique photographs. These data are a visual reconstruction, not measured BIM.

## Level 1

Photo 8 is the common coordinate grid. Room boundaries were visually traced, preserving the north technology workshops, CPP suite east, administration southeast, open courtyard west of cafeteria, and rotated triple gym southwest. Three gym volumes, cafeteria and the four major north workshops (transportation, manufacturing, innovation, construction) use inferred 8.1 m height. North workshop double-height is corroborated by Level 2 shop voids and occupied Level 3 sciences above. Drama/stage is also treated as a tall performance volume; this is less certain than the gyms. Other rooms use inferred 3.85 m height. Stair A/B/C/D follow the common anchors. Five circulation polygons express connected main routes; door openings and exact corridor wall thickness are unresolved. Tiny support rooms and administrative offices are approximate; compression and perspective obscure their labels.

## Level 0

Photo 10 supports five childcare teaching rooms (two toddler, infant, two preschool) plus sleep, servery, staff and support spaces. The rest of the building is shaded as outside this level. Childcare is placed only below the western gym wing. An approximate affine registration maps Photo 10 coordinates to Photo 8 using the western gym/Stair D relationship. Photo perspective differs strongly, so all Level 0 polygons are marked inferred. Stair D centroids were subsequently aligned by a +1.145,+14.787 pixel translation. Exact perimeter registration, stair dimensions and exterior playground boundaries remain unresolved. Exterior playgrounds are not modeled as indoor rooms.

## Limits

Coordinates are photographic pixels, not surveyed coordinates. The shared 0.14 m/pixel conversion is a visualization assumption. Elevations -4.2 m and 0 m are inferred. No exact area, egress width, accessibility compliance or construction accuracy is claimed. Adjacent polygons preserve room relationships, but small photographic gaps/overlaps can exist.

Three separate Photo 10 exterior playgrounds (toddler, infant, preschool) are available in `src/data/childcare-site.json`, using the childcare registration and inferred -4.2 m elevation.
