# Upper-level tracing

Photo 4 (Level 2) and Photo 5 (Level 3) were traced remotely into the common Photo 8 pixel grid with four-point projective registration. Illustrative reconstruction of undimensioned photographed drawings; not survey/BIM accuracy.

Level 2 includes west classrooms, north sanitary/support core, learning commons/seminars/open stair, east classrooms, student success, exercise/dance above change rooms, and Stair B. Level 3 includes northwest science/prep, north general science/physics, sanitary core, commons mezzanine, east classrooms, communication technology/TV, art/kiln/storage, computer/creative/photography and south washroom.

One connected hall/co-lab polygon per level links north and south wings. Stair D is reached through the exercise room, rather than the main co-lab. Dashed gym/shop/cafeteria areas are explicit voids, and L3 gym/exercise roofs are not occupied slabs.

Labels unresolved: southwest Level 3 science and the narrow Level 2 north service room. Generic labels are intentional. General science 1–3 are model identifiers, not source room numbers. Wall thickness, doors, stair flight direction, exact dimensions and structural grid remain unverified. Stair A/B/C register closely across both upper levels. Stair D traces the small labeled northern portion of the west stair structure; its centroid differs from the broad canonical anchor.

Small support-room footprints take precedence where hand traces overlap larger rooms. Circulation is clipped to the room boundaries; tiny disconnected edge fragments are removed. Roof/void boundaries are clipped against occupied footprints. Shapely QA checks polygon validity and pairwise occupied room/circulation/void overlaps. Rounding to 0.01 image pixel is numerical precision, not measurement accuracy.
